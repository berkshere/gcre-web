SYSTEMATIC MACRO ASSET ALLOCATION

How GCRE Works

A systematic framework for translating changes in the investment environment into asset allocation decisions.

01 The Problem and What is GCRE?

Markets Change. Portfolios Often Don't.

Markets do not remain in the same investment environment.

Growth changes. Inflation changes. Monetary conditions change. Market risk changes. The relationships between asset classes can change with them.

Yet many investment portfolios are built around relatively stable assumptions about how assets should behave.

For investors who actively allocate across macro-sensitive assets, the challenge is not simply deciding what to own.

The harder question is:

When the investment environment changes, how should portfolio exposure change with it?

What is GCRE?

GCRE — Global Capital Regime Engine — is a systematic macro asset allocation engine designed to translate changes in the investment environment into systematic portfolio decisions.

GCRE does not attempt to predict every daily asset price movement.

Instead, it evaluates the broader investment environment, determines which assets are appropriate for the current regime, adjusts exposure according to asset-level risk, and evaluates how that exposure should be expressed within portfolio constraints.

The objective is to systematically adjust cross-asset risk exposure as the investment environment changes.

02 The GCRE Decision Process

From Market Conditions to Portfolio Decisions

GCRE separates portfolio construction into distinct decision layers.

Each layer answers a different question. Macro conditions determine the environment. The regime determines the opportunity set. Asset-level risk determines exposure intensity. Position sizing converts those decisions into portfolio weights. Instrument selection determines how the exposure is expressed. Portfolio constraints determine what can ultimately be held.

Each layer has a defined role. No single signal is responsible for the entire portfolio decision.

03 Macro Regime

What Environment Are We In?

The Macro Regime layer evaluates the broader investment environment rather than attempting to predict individual asset prices.

GCRE evaluates several independent dimensions of the environment, including economic conditions, monetary conditions, market conditions and crisis risk.

These inputs are combined into a systematic macro regime classification.

04 Asset Selection

05 Base Allocation

What Is Normal Exposure?

For each macro regime, GCRE defines a base allocation representing the portfolio's normal-risk exposure to the eligible assets.

The base allocation provides the starting point for position sizing before asset-level risk adjustments are applied.

Base Allocation is not Final Allocation.

The base portfolio describes what the portfolio would hold under normal risk conditions. Asset-level risk and timing conditions can subsequently increase, reduce or remove that exposure.

06 Asset Risk & Timing

How Much Exposure?

Macro conditions can remain unchanged while the risk of an individual asset changes.

GCRE therefore evaluates asset-level risk and timing independently from the macro regime.

Changes in volatility, price behavior and other asset-level conditions can adjust the appropriate exposure without requiring a change in the broader macro regime.

07 Capital Efficiency

How Should Exposure Be Expressed?

Once an underlying exposure has been justified, GCRE can evaluate how that exposure should be expressed.

Depending on the risk and timing conditions, the system may use a conventional or leveraged instrument to express the same underlying exposure.

Equity Growth
QQQ → TQQQ
Treasury / Duration
TLT → TMF
Energy / Commodity
USO → UCO

Leveraged instruments are not treated as independent directional signals.

The underlying exposure must first be justified by the allocation process. Leverage is then evaluated as a capital-efficiency decision subject to risk and portfolio constraints.

Leverage does not decide what to own. It determines how an approved exposure may be expressed.

08 Portfolio Construction

What Do We Actually Hold?

The individual allocation decisions are combined into a portfolio and passed through portfolio-level constraints.

These constraints may include position limits, leverage limits, minimum defensive exposure, asset eligibility and other portfolio risk requirements.

09 Continuous Evaluation

Does the Process Work?

GCRE records its daily decisions so that the behavior of the framework can be evaluated over time.

Each allocation decision can be traced back to the macro regime, asset selection, risk conditions, position sizing and instrument selection that produced it.

Outcomes can then be evaluated through performance, risk and attribution analysis.

Parameters may be calibrated and tested as the system evolves. Changes to the underlying methodology are treated separately and require independent validation.

Historical outcomes do not retroactively change historical decisions.

10 Transparency

GCRE is designed around systematic, repeatable and auditable decision processes.

The platform publishes portfolio positioning, macro conditions, risk assessments, daily reports and historical outcomes so that the behavior of the framework can be evaluated over time.

The objective is not to present a collection of market opinions, but to make the decision process observable and reproducible.

The decision process matters as much as the outcome.
