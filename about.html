ABOUT GCRE

Global Capital Regime Engine

What is GCRE?
GCRE stands for Global Capital Regime Engine .

GCRE is a systematic investment framework designed to evaluate macroeconomic regimes, market risk, liquidity conditions, asset trends and timing signals.

The framework dynamically adjusts portfolio exposure according to changing market conditions rather than relying on a static asset allocation.
Mission & Investment Philosophy
GCRE was developed to address a fundamental challenge in global investing: capital regimes continuously change over time.

Traditional asset allocation models often rely on fixed assumptions about risk, growth, inflation and market relationships.

GCRE takes a different approach. It treats markets as dynamic systems where macroeconomic conditions, liquidity, volatility and investor behavior interact.

The objective of GCRE is not to predict every market movement, but to identify changing investment environments and adjust exposure systematically.

Research Methodology
GCRE combines multiple analytical layers into a systematic decision framework.

1. Macro Regime Analysis
Evaluates economic growth conditions, inflation trends, interest-rate expectations, financial stress and liquidity environment.

2. Market Risk Analysis
Measures volatility conditions through indicators including VIX, MOVE, credit spreads and market stress signals.

3. Asset Trend & Timing Analysis
Analyzes price trends, momentum, volatility structure and cycle conditions across multiple asset classes.

4. Portfolio Construction
Transforms research signals into dynamic portfolio allocation while considering risk control and capital preservation.
