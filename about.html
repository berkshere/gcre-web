Global Capital Regime Engine

What is GCRE?
GCRE stands for Global Capital Regime Engine .

GCRE is a systematic investment framework designed to evaluate macroeconomic regimes, market risk, liquidity conditions, asset trends and timing signals.

The framework dynamically adjusts portfolio exposure according to changing market conditions rather than relying on a static asset allocation.
Investment Framework
01
Macro Regime
Identify the prevailing economic, liquidity and market-risk regime.
02
Timing Engine
Evaluate asset-level trend, momentum and volatility conditions to determine timing signals.
03
Portfolio Allocation
Translate macro and timing signals into dynamic portfolio exposure.
04
Risk Control
Adjust exposure according to market volatility, drawdown and changing risk conditions.
05
Daily Reporting
Generate a daily systematic report covering macro regime, portfolio positioning and market risk.
