UNDERSTANDING THE MODELS

Two Portfolio Models, One GCRE Premium Product

GCRE Premium provides portfolio decisions through its portfolio models. BASE and PREMIUM are model configurations, not separate products or subscription levels.

BASE PORTFOLIO MODEL

Core Portfolio

A lower-complexity portfolio model designed around established assets and systematic market adaptation.

PREMIUM PORTFOLIO MODEL

Extended Portfolio

A broader portfolio model that can incorporate additional portfolio capabilities and leverage assets where appropriate.
