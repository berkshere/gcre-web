RISK MANAGEMENT

How to Reduce Portfolio Drawdown

Long-term investing requires not only seeking returns, but also managing risk when market conditions change.

Why Portfolio Drawdowns Matter

Long-term investors often focus on returns, but large losses can significantly affect long-term compounding. A portfolio decline requires a larger recovery to return to the previous level.

A 20% decline requires a 25% recovery to return to the original value.

A 50% decline requires a 100% recovery.

The Challenge of Risk Management

Many investors recognize risk only after significant market declines have already occurred. At that point, decisions are often influenced by fear rather than a systematic process.

Effective risk management requires monitoring changing conditions before a major drawdown happens.

Why Fixed Allocation Can Be Challenging

Traditional portfolios often assume that historical relationships between assets will remain stable. However, economic and market conditions can change.

Growth, inflation, liquidity, interest rates and volatility can influence how different assets behave.

Risk Management Is About Adaptation

The purpose of a systematic framework is not to eliminate uncertainty. Markets cannot be predicted with certainty.

The goal is to create a repeatable process for evaluating when conditions are changing and when portfolio exposure may need adjustment.
