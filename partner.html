Who We Are Looking For

Partner requirements.

The ideal partner combines local access with professional credibility and the ability to explain a systematic investment framework accurately. We value execution, integrity, and long-term alignment over the size of a contact list alone.

Core Requirements

What a strong partner brings

  • Local market knowledge: understands the investment landscape, customer needs, business practices, and relevant local rules.
  • Relevant professional network: can establish credible relationships with investment professionals, institutions, financial businesses, or qualified investor groups.
  • Business development capability: can identify prospects, arrange introductory discussions, and follow opportunities through.
  • Professional communication: can explain GCRE's purpose, methodology, and limitations without exaggeration.
  • Integrity and compliance awareness: respects confidentiality, avoids misleading claims, and understands the need for local legal and regulatory review.
  • Long-term commitment: is prepared to provide market feedback and develop relationships consistently.

Preferred Background

Relevant experience and resources

  • Investment research, asset management, wealth management, or financial planning experience.
  • Financial technology, market data, quantitative research, or investment software experience.
  • Established relationships with financial professionals, investor communities, or potential institutional collaborators.
  • Experience in business development, strategic partnerships, or distribution of professional financial products.
  • Local language capability and an understanding of regional business culture.
  • Ability to organize meetings, maintain a qualified opportunity pipeline, and report market feedback clearly.
A partner does not need to possess every preferred qualification. GCRE will assess the applicant's experience, local access, proposed role, and relevant regulatory considerations individually. Any required professional authorization must be confirmed before regulated activities are undertaken.

Partner Responsibilities

What regional partners are expected to do.

Regional partners help GCRE understand local markets, establish the right relationships, and evaluate opportunities for responsible growth. The exact scope will be agreed in writing before work begins.

01 / MARKET DEVELOPMENT

Build the local market

Identify where GCRE may be relevant and develop a focused understanding of potential customer and institutional needs.

  • Identify suitable market segments.
  • Map potential customers and organizations.
  • Research competing solutions and local needs.
02 / RELATIONSHIPS

Develop qualified introductions

Establish professional relationships and connect GCRE with relevant people or organizations.

  • Arrange introductory meetings.
  • Explain GCRE's intended use and positioning.
  • Track follow-ups and next steps.
03 / PRODUCT FEEDBACK

Represent local user needs

Help GCRE understand what local users need to evaluate the product and what information remains unclear.

  • Collect structured feedback.
  • Identify localization requirements.
  • Report common objections and questions.
04 / PRODUCT EDUCATION

Explain the framework accurately

Use GCRE-approved materials to explain its systematic approach and the meaning of its research and portfolio outputs.

  • Use approved presentations and materials.
  • Explain historical results in context.
  • Escalate technical questions when needed.
05 / COMPLIANCE COORDINATION

Support local assessment

Help identify local requirements before any marketing, distribution, or regulated activity is undertaken.

  • Flag relevant local rules and restrictions.
  • Coordinate with qualified local advisers.
  • Avoid unauthorized financial promotions.
06 / BUSINESS EXECUTION

Maintain a working plan

Work toward agreed milestones and maintain transparent communication with GCRE.

  • Agree target segments and activity goals.
  • Provide periodic opportunity updates.
  • Review progress and refine the market plan.

Working Together

Clear responsibilities. Clear boundaries.

GCRE seeks partners who can develop the market without compromising the integrity of its research, product communication, or operating principles.

Market Development

Partners develop local relationships and introduce relevant opportunities. Specific sales targets, territory rights, and commercial terms are agreed separately.

Product Integrity

Partners must not independently change GCRE's research methodology, model outputs, or official product claims.

Responsible Communication

No guaranteed-return claims, unsupported performance claims, or representations that GCRE can eliminate investment risk.

Regulatory Boundaries

Partners must not provide investment advice, manage assets, solicit restricted products, or conduct other regulated activities unless properly authorized and specifically agreed.

Partner status does not automatically confer exclusivity, authority to represent GCRE legally, access to confidential systems, or permission to use GCRE's name and materials beyond the agreed scope. These matters require explicit written agreement.

Application Process

From initial contact to a defined partnership.

GCRE will evaluate each opportunity based on market fit, partner capabilities, proposed responsibilities, and practical implementation requirements.

STEP 01

Submit Your Profile

STEP 02

Evaluate Market Fit

Discuss local opportunities, target users, available resources, and the proposed role.

STEP 03

Define the Scope

Agree on responsibilities, milestones, permitted activities, commercial arrangements, and applicable compliance requirements.

STEP 04

Begin Collaboration

Start with an agreed market plan and review progress through regular communication.
